Interview Notes

  • flex-grow: How much to GROW when extra space (0 = no grow, 1 = grow)
  • flex-shrink: How much to SHRINK when less space (0 = don't shrink)
  • flex-basis: Starting size before grow/shrink
  • flex: 1 = shorthand - most used in real projects!
  • Yesterday: justify-content + align-items = position
  • Today: flex-grow/shrink/basis = size
